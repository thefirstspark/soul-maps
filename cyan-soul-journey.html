The Channel
The signal moves through you with less resistance now. You did not invent what comes out of your mouth at 3am. You did not write the song that arrived whole. You are the wire. The job is not to make the signal louder — only to stop pinching the line.
You Are Here

You cannot take full credit and you cannot fully refuse it. Something arrives through you that does not exactly belong to you, but it could not arrive without you. That tension is the price of being cyan. The signal does not need you to push it — it needs you to stop apologizing for being the place it lands.
