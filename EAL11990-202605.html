Capricorn Suns are especially vulnerable to this machinery because they are wired to take authority seriously — they internalize verdicts. You can hold someone accountable for the choices they made and still be furious at the system that punished those choices with a life sentence delivered fifteen minutes at a time.

09 · The Ritual

For You, KJP

The Two Truths Practice
For carrying someone whole without carrying them forward

Intent: To hold both the love and the line without collapsing them into each other.

What You Need

  • Two small pieces of paper
  • One pen
  • A candle if you want one
  • Something fireproof for the second paper

The Practice

  1. On the first paper: write one specific thing you loved about him. One sentence. Concrete. Not a paragraph.
  2. On the second paper: write one specific thing he did that cost you. One sentence. Concrete.
  3. Hold one in each hand. Say out loud: "Both of these are true. Neither one cancels the other. I am allowed to know both."
  4. Keep the first paper. Put it somewhere you can find it later — not the front of the drawer. The back.
  5. Burn the second. Not to forgive it. To mark that you've already metabolized it. The information lives in you now. You don't need to keep the page.
  6. Close: "I'm just me, KJP. Right here, right now. He is over there. The line between us is real."

What to Watch For

This isn't about getting over him. It's about moving him from the foreground to the archive — still accessible, no longer running in the background all the time. Some loves don't fully leave. They just take less of the room as the years pass. This practice marks that the room has already started to open back up.
