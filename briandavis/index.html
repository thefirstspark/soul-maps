The 8-Gate Protocol

A ritual calibrated to your Personal Year 8

Do this on the night of a new moon, Nov 2026 if possible (closest to your solar return). Needs: paper, pen, a candle, 20 quiet minutes.

  1. Light the candle. Write the number 8 at the top of the page.
  2. Below it, write: "What have I built that I haven't claimed?" Answer in one line. Don't edit.
  3. Write: "What am I pretending not to know about my own power?" Answer in one line.
