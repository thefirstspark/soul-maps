Will Meets Mind
Red and blue held in tension — the soul contains both drives without resolving them. This is the most complex and most painful configuration. You see too clearly and feel too much. But you also build what others can only imagine. You point at things nobody else can name.
You Are Here

At purple, something else arrives alongside the color — the part that watches without participating. Not the red that acts. Not the blue that thinks. The part that simply observes the whole arc and knows it has been here before. This is the Witness. It does not speak. It remembers.
