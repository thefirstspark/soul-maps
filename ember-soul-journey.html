You · Now
Current · The Ember Life
Keeper of the Coal
You hold a fire someone else lit. You did not start it and you cannot let it go out. This is the burden and the gift of being an ember — the flame is older than your face, and it chose to keep burning through you. You are the current chapter of a much longer book.
You Are Here

Something older than your face passes through you. When you cook a recipe you were never taught, when a word in a language you don't speak rises up in a dream, when a stranger says you look exactly like — that is the ember reporting in. You are not haunted. You are inhabited by the ones who refused to disappear.

The Hand-Off
A moment arrives — a birth, a deathbed, a teaching, a story finally told — when the fire moves out of you and into someone younger. You don't lose it. You just stop being the only one carrying it.
