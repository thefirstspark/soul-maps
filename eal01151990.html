08 · The Ritual

For You, KJP — Not For Him

The Two-Truths Protocol
For loving someone and also being done with them

Intent: Hold both truths in the same hand without trying to resolve them.

Materials

  • Two small pieces of paper
  • One pen
  • A candle (optional)
  • A bowl or small fireproof dish

Steps

  1. On the first paper, write one true thing about him you still love. One. Specific. Not a paragraph.
  2. On the second paper, write one true thing he did that hurt you. Also specific. Also one.
  3. Hold both papers, one in each hand. Say out loud: "Both of these are true. Neither cancels the other. I am allowed to know both."
  4. Keep the first paper somewhere you can find it later. Tuck it in a book, slip it in a drawer.
  5. Burn the second paper. Not to forgive him — to mark that you've already metabolized it. The information is in you now. You don't need the page anymore.
  6. Close with: "I'm just me, KJP. Right here. Right now. He is over there. The line between us is real."

What to notice

This isn't about getting over him. It's about being able to remember him without bleeding. Some loves don't fully leave. They just take less of the room over time. This ritual moves him from the foreground to the file cabinet.
