Frequently Asked

Do I need my exact birth time?

An exact birth time gives the most complete reading — particularly for your Ascendant, House placements, and Human Design gates. If you don't know it, note that in your intake form. We can generate a chart focused on the elements that don't require it, or work from an approximation if you have one.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery is within 48 hours of purchase. The Origin tier ships within 24 hours. You'll receive a confirmation email immediately after checkout, then your PDF when it's ready. No manual processing windows — the system runs around the clock.

Is this AI-generated or personalized?

Your charts are cast from your actual birth data — not pulled from a template. The Soul Map synthesis layer is built specifically for The First Spark and produces output unique to your configuration. This isn't a generic sun-sign reading.

Is there a live call or voice memo included?

No — intentionally. This model is built to be scalable and on your schedule. You get a PDF built for deep reading and rereading, plus portal access — not a one-time call you have to schedule. If you want to go deeper after your map, the Sparkverse community is the next step.

What's the Sparkverse portal?

Sparkverse is The First Spark's membership layer — an interactive portal that extends your Soul Map into tools, community, and ongoing content. Origin tier includes Inner Sanctum access. Full Map and Spark tiers receive standard portal access.
