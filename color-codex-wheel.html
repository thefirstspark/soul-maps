The Color Codex

The wheel that picks your color

Every Soul Map lands on exactly one slice. Your Radiant Number is your Life Path plus your Expression, reduced. It lands on one of nine slices. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are not slices of their own: they reduce to 2, 4 and 6 and wear that colour Moonsilvered, tinted toward white, with the master ring.

Tap a slice to read that colour's soul journey

01
Life Path

Your birth date, digit by digit, reduced to a single number. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept whole.

02
Expression

Your full birth name, letter by letter, reduced the same way. This is the vessel you arrived in.

03
Radiant

Add the two and reduce once more. The result is the slice you land on. It is fixed at birth and does not change.

The nine slices

Slice order runs clockwise from the top. Masters 11, 22 and 33 reduce to 2, 4 and 6 and wear that colour Moonsilvered (tinted toward white) with the master ring. Ruled 2026-09-05.
