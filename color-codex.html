The Color Codex

Every color is a frequency.

Soul maps aren't sorted by personality. They're sorted by the frequency a soul vibrates at — the color it was born. The codex is the key to the whole spectrum.

// How to read this

The color on a soul map is the frequency you were born — your core color, drawn from your birth numerology. It does not change. The arc of how you shift across a lifetime — current, transition, shadow — lives inside each individual map.

Nine colors form the light spectrum, from Red at the root to White at the crown. Two more — Rose and Silver — are structural frequencies: they map to layers of the Selector Model rather than to visible light.

Tiers marked live already hold mapped souls. Tiers marked reserved are real frequencies awaiting their first soul — the spectrum is complete even where the archive is still filling.
