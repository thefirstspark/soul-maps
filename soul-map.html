Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to know my exact birth time?

An exact birth time gives the most precise reading, particularly for your Ascendant and House placements. If you don't know it, we can work with an approximate time or create a chart focused on the elements that don't require it. Just let me know in the intake form.

How long does delivery take?

The Spark reading is typically delivered within 3–4 business days. The Full Map takes 5–7 business days given the depth of work involved. You'll receive email confirmation once your reading is underway.

Is this a real, personalised reading?

Absolutely. Every Soul Map is written personally for you — not generated by software. Your charts inform the framework, and the narrative is crafted specifically around your unique configuration.

What if I've already had a birth chart reading?

A Soul Map goes much further. Rather than interpreting one system in isolation, it synthesises astrology, Human Design, and numerology into a unified picture — often revealing layers that single-system readings miss entirely.

Can I gift a Soul Map?

Yes — gifting a Soul Map is one of the most thoughtful things you can do. Simply select your preferred reading at checkout and indicate it is a gift. You'll receive a beautiful gift card you can share in your own time.
