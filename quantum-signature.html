004 · Questions

What you'll want to know.

I don't know anything about crypto. Do I need a wallet?

No. We use Crossmint — you pay with a credit card, and a wallet is created and held for you automatically. You'll never see gas fees, MetaMask, or seed phrases unless you want to. The chain is the receipt; you don't have to learn the post office.

What does "soulbound" actually mean?

Soulbound means the credential is permanently tied to your wallet and cannot be transferred, sold, or flipped. It's the opposite of a Bored Ape. Real-world equivalents: a diploma, a passport, a birth certificate. It proves something is yours — and the proof can't be moved to someone else.

What happens to my Signature when I die?

This is the most important question. Default behavior: the token enters memorial mode — the metadata flips to honor the soul, the wallet stays bound, but descendants can mint a linked credential into a Bloodline tree. The Signature becomes part of your lineage, on-chain, viewable forever. You aren't traded. You are remembered.

Why $33 deposit instead of full payment?

Because the contract isn't deployed yet. The deposit validates that founder demand exists before we ship — and protects you in case we don't. If we hit 50 founder deposits, we deploy. If we don't, every deposit is refunded in full. No risk to you, no premature spend on our end.

Can I add to my Signature later?

Yes. The base Signature uses dynamic metadata — when you add a Mind Map, Bloodline, or Family Constellation, the chain reflects the new layer. You can also mint companion tokens for major additions, each linked to your parent token. One soul, an evolving on-chain artifact.

It's named in the footer like a hidden door, and the people who find it self-select. The artifact reveals itself to those who look.
