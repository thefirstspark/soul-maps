Date Uncertainty
The exact birth date of Jesus of Nazareth is not historically recoverable. Scholarly consensus places the birth between approximately 6 and 4 BC, before the death of Herod the Great (ca. 4 BC). The specific date April 17, 6 BC is used here because it corresponds to a Jupiter occultation of Aries visible from Babylon, proposed by astronomer Michael Molnar as a candidate for the astronomical event recorded in the Nativity account. Other defensible estimates include Spring 4 BC and various dates in 5 BC. No birth time is historically recorded. Numerology uses year 6 as the digit; Chinese zodiac uses astronomical year −5. All readings carry the full weight of this uncertainty and should be read accordingly.
