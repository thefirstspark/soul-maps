## Repo notes
- Customer maps are `[initials][mmddyyyy].html`, generated by the engine in the PRIVATE repo `thefirstspark/soul-map-engine` (soul_map_generator.py lives there since 2026-09-05); monthly updates are `-YYYYMM.html`. Never hand-edit a generated map's numbers.
- `signatures.json` is the permanent Quantum Signature registry (first mint wins). Never regenerate or reorder it.
- Color Codex source of truth is each map's badge; `codex-colors.css` holds the palette. Issued colour is also pinned on the mint in `signatures.json` as `codex_color` + `codex_version` (`radiant-wheel-v1`). Never recompute a minted colour. Never reprint a map to move its colour.
- `card.html` (?p1/?p2) is what Frequency Match links to.
